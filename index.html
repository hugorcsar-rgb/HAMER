THE FOUNDERS

Three Division I golfers. Three operators. One firm.

Javier, Hugo and Diego played NCAA Division I golf. After college they moved into consulting, operations and sports industry roles — and saw the same gap from every angle: ambitious junior golfers navigating recruiting and development with templates from the internet, and camps building programs without a real framework. HAMER is the firm they wish their families had hired when they were sixteen.
